Privacy Policy

NosePrint · Last updated November 20, 2026

In short: NosePrint works entirely on your device. We don't collect, upload, sell or share your photos or designs. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Photos and camera

NosePrint asks for access to your camera or photo library only when you choose to take or pick a photo of your dog's nose. That photo is processed on your device to create line art. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Saving your designs

Designs you keep in “My Designs” are stored locally in the app's storage on your device. If you choose to save an image to your photo library or to Files, NosePrint asks for permission to add it there. Deleting the app removes the designs stored inside it.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your device's standard share sheet opens and you choose where the image goes (for example messaging or email apps). What happens after that is governed by the app you share to.

App updates

To deliver bug fixes, the app checks for updates using Expo's update service (EAS Update). This request includes technical information such as the app version, platform and update channel, and it does not include your photos or designs. See Expo's privacy policy.

Information we don't collect

  • No personal information, names, emails or accounts
  • No location data
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers
  • No microphone access

Children

NosePrint does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
